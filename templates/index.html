How to play

Players take turns drawing their mark in an empty square of a 3 by 3 grid. Get three of your marks in a row, in a column or on a diagonal, and you win. If all nine squares fill up with no line, it is a draw.

Who is on the other side of the paper

Easy: random

It chooses any free square, with no plan at all.

Medium: rules of thumb

Win now if possible. Otherwise block you. Otherwise take the center, then a corner, then an edge.

Unbeatable: Minimax

It imagines every way the game could go, assumes you play perfectly, and picks the move with the best worst case. Alpha-Beta pruning lets it skip moves that cannot matter.
